# Risk of Deadlock

Play Risk of Rain 2 as **Haze, Abrams, Seven, Infernus, Mo & Krill, Viscous, Rat King, Billy, Wraith, Mina, Pocket, Victor or Paige**, with their models, animations, guns, abilities, icons and voices.

> Every hero's files are built **into the DLL**: Deadlock does not need to be installed.

- **Discord:** https://discord.gg/sNByetmcqc
- **Balance pages:** [Balance overview](https://claude.ai/artifact/5zx1qdr3LnXL2vhzJiebo3). These are just design sheets: how I make sure every hero is balanced for the Risk of Rain 2 universe.

## Requirements
- Built with Risk of Rain 2 build 25921972 (2026-11-10).
- Risk of Rain 2 on Steam, with **BepInExPack** (bbepis, 5.4.2122) and **RoR2BepInExPack** (RiskofThunder, 1.49.0 or newer). r2modman lists both as this package's dependencies.
- No Deadlock install needed. The first start of a new DLL unpacks its files in a few seconds.

## Installation
- **r2modman:** Settings → Profile → **Import local mod** → this zip (author JakeHornerMan), then **Start modded**. Remove any older Risk of Deadlock first: only one copy.
- **By hand (Steam's Play button):** BepInExPack in the game folder, RoR2BepInExPack in `BepInEx\plugins\RiskofThunder-RoR2BepInExPack`, then this package in `BepInEx\plugins\JakeHornerMan-Risk_of_Deadlock`.
- Every step, updating and removing everything: **plugins/INSTALL.txt** in this package. What's inside it: **plugins/About this package.txt**. What changed in each version: **plugins/CHANGELOG.txt**. The Discord invite: **plugins/Join Discord.txt**.

## Features
- Thirteen new survivors, each with Risk of Rain 2's four skills built from their Deadlock kit, plus their ultimate as equipment:
  - **Left Click:** their Deadlock gun, with its magazine and reload (the rounds show above the skill bar).
  - **Right Click and Shift:** two Deadlock abilities.
  - **R:** a fourth move from their Deadlock kit, items or melee.
  - **Q (equipment):** their Deadlock ultimate. Every hero starts the run holding their own; anyone can find the ults in chests. The pickup is a little figure of the hero.
- **Haze:** Twin SMGs, Sleep Dagger, Smoke Bomb, Phantom Strike; Q Bullet Dance. Fixation stacks on every hit and bursts.
- **Abrams:** Shotgun, Siphon Life, Shoulder Charge, Haymaker; Q Seismic Impact.
- **Seven:** Shock Rifle, Lightning Ball, Static Charge, Storm Cloud; Q Thunderhead.
- **Infernus:** Flame Gun, Napalm, Flame Dash, Torment Pulse; Q Concussive Combustion. Hits build Afterburn.
- **Mo & Krill:** Krill's shotgun, Sand Blast, Burrow, Tag Team; Q Dust Devil.
- **Viscous:** goo blobs, Splatter, The Cube, Puddle Punch; his puddles top up his ammo; Q Goo Ball.
- **Rat King:** Sovereign's Decree, Scrap Grenade, Royal Pestments, Rule, Ratannia!; Q Rat Swarm.
- **Billy:** Biquette, the Bat combo, Bashdown, Rising Ram; a goring air dodge, no fall damage; Q Chain Gang.
- **Wraith:** The Rake, Card Trick, Project Mind, Full Auto; Q Telekinesis.
- **Mina:** New Money, Rake, Sanguine Retreat, **Nox Nostra** (a cone of bats pours onto a ringed circle under your crosshair); Q Mist Form. Hold Jump to float under her umbrella; Love Bites bring her moves back sooner.
- **Pocket:** The Black Sheep, Barrage, Flying Cloak, Enchanter's Satchel; Q **Frog Form** (the case pops open and you leap out as a frog; every landing, on the ground or on an enemy, slams and stuns). One jump plus an air dash.
- **Victor:** **Memento Mori** on Left Click (a gun that spins up while you hold it), **Jumpstart** on Right Click (shock yourself for 10 health: +1 Pain and +30 % move speed for 5 s), Pain Battery, **Aura of Suffering** (a toggle that hurts you and siphons everything near you, growing with Pain); Q **Shocking Reanimation** (rise into a lightning strike that stuns everything within 30 m, then 20 s of 10 Ukuleles and 10 Tesla Coils). Every enemy hit you take adds a **Pain** stack: +15 % attack speed each, up to 10.
- **Paige:** **Chapter's End** on Left Click (glowing spirit pages that pierce up to 3 enemies), **Bookwyrm** on Right Click (her ghostly wyvern flies through the target breathing green fire on the floor, leaving it burning), **Captivating Read** on Shift (a magic circle that slows, then her giant spirit sword slams down and immobilizes), **Rallying Charge** on R (a row of 5 spectral knights charges, trampling and stunning enemies and healing allies); Q **The Green Knight** (one giant green knight that grows stronger the further it rides). **Plot Armor:** +25 % damage while you're below 40 % health. Every spirit is see-through ghost green.
- Deadlock voice lines at the right moments; co-op with up to 4 players (everyone needs the same build).
